## What's New in v0.10.1

Full Mindmap now lists the direct files and folders of a folder, up to 10,000 items. This fixes folders that looked empty or "not indexed" when they were under that limit.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Folders no longer look "not indexed"** (MDV-044, #69): Full Mindmap lists a folder's direct files and folders, up to a 10,000-item scan limit per folder. It works even when the folder sits on a very big tree such as `/`. Before, some folders showed "not indexed" because the scan ran out of budget before it reached them.
- **Huge folders open in pages** (MDV-044, #69): A folder with more than 2,000 items shows the first 2,000 and a **More (N left)** node. Select it and press Enter to see the next 2,000. The map stays fast, and memory use is equal or lower.

### ⚠️ Known Limits

- Folder totals on very large trees can still show `N+` (a lower bound). Only the direct list is complete.
- A folder with more than 10,000 direct items still shows **More items not indexed**. Items past the 10,000th are not listed in Full Mindmap.
- Hidden files count toward the scan budget before the "show hidden files" filter hides them, so a folder with thousands of hidden files can show fewer visible files than expected.
